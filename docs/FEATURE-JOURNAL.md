reason important features changed. It complements the factual release log in
[`../CHANGELOG.md`](../CHANGELOG.md). It must not be replaced by short-lived chat history, a pull
request description, or an issue comment.

## 2026-11-07 — Treat intelTHAT as the application it is

**Founder correction:** intelTHAT is an actual app and must perform as one. A
renamed panel with fetch buttons and receipt cards is not the product.

**Implementation:** Added a dedicated `/intelthat/` application with its own
Research Cockpit, Market Pack, Macro Conditions, News Intelligence, SEC
Monitor, and Run History rooms. A research run resolves supported company names
to symbols, shows measured elapsed time, settles the four source readers
independently, and keeps formula, inputs, timeframe, timestamp, provenance,
freshness, confidence, and whole-receipt export attached to every result.
Browser-local saved runs restore the exact captured receipt. The former Market
Compass room is now honestly labeled as a bridge into the app.

**Authority boundary:** Application status does not grant trading authority.
intelTHAT generates research evidence and signals only. Broker OAuth,
credentials, account data, orders, execution readiness as authority, position
management, trade recording, money movement, performance promises, and Market
Compass score influence remain permanently refused.

## 2026-11-05 — Design triggers, restraint, approvals and calculateTHAT

**Founder direction:** Make “when to go” and “when not to go” explicit, keep every concern in a micro-slice, and add calculateTHAT.

**Implementation:** Separated trigger observation, transparent calculation, Actor psychology, approval ownership and execution into independent contracts. Hard stops outrank positive triggers. The app may explain approval readiness but cannot grant approval or execute a trade.

**Authority boundary:** Trading and score influence remain off. An approval is exact, external, expiring and separately owned; it cannot be manufactured by a positive calculation.

## 2026-11-05 — Make the compass boundary permanent and signals active

**Founder correction:** Market Compass will never trade. It comprises measuring tools, calculators, indicators, Actor preferences and psychology rules that help a person make prudent decisions.

**Implementation:** Go, Watch and Do Not Go are active research signals. Signal generation no longer waits for a trade-approval receipt. calculateTHAT reads the evidence score without mutating it, shows the direction and evidence balance, and keeps broker execution permanently outside the product.

**Product boundary:** Market Compass measures, calculates, compares, warns and points. The human decides and acts elsewhere.

## v0.1–v0.2 — Establish the evidence system and its visible report

**Intention:** Answer more than “is a technical indicator bullish?” by building a two-sided,
explainable evidence system around asset reality, structure, momentum, routes, memory, news,
history, relationships, narrative, forecasting, and invalidation.

**Product principle:** Each feature remains a named product/IP node. The evidence balance is not a
calibrated probability, history is not a guarantee, inferred links are not verified causation, and
the system actively searches for counter-evidence.

[`IP-NODE-REGISTRY.md`](IP-NODE-REGISTRY.md), and the v0.1/v0.2 implementation history.
