# Market Compass v0.3 Workbench

v0.3 promotes the product from a report-style dashboard to an interactive trading workbench.

Implemented in this release:

- Decision Cockpit with action, evidence, confidence, current price, next Bus Stop, invalidation, and reward/risk.
- Interactive candlestick chart using Lightweight Charts when the CDN is available.
- EMA 13, 27, 50, and 81 overlays.
- Fibonacci retracement/extension levels, anchor dates, confluence detection, support/resistance, Bus Stops, and invalidation on the price chart.
- Dedicated RSI 14 panel with visible 30 / 50 / 70 lines, crossings, zone, and simple divergence detection.
- Dedicated MACD panel with line, signal, and histogram.
- Best-effort 4H / 1D / 1W analysis with timeframe agreement feeding trend confidence.
- Evidence contribution breakdown after confidence and dependence discounts.
- Price Memory heatmap cards with test count, age, span, reaction strength, erosion, and distance.
- Local browser watchlist and saved analysis snapshots.
- Recent catalyst timeline from the available news feed.
- Evidence Board now distinguishes verified market-metadata context from inferred news co-mentions.
- Historical analog outcome context is surfaced explicitly as context, not a calibrated probability.

The application still does not place trades, claim causal relationships from co-mentions, or label evidence balance as profit probability.
