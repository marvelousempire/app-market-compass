# Asset Channel

The main Market Compass canvas has an Asset Channel room. It follows the selected market symbol and lets a person switch among the existing quick-watch symbols. Articles and videos appear only when the source record explicitly associates the exact resolved ticker and supplies a secure source link. Publisher, publication time and source link remain visible. A missing feed produces an explicit empty state. The channel's Sources view shows feed provenance; the Settings room owns saved per-asset publisher display choices.

SEC filings use the existing EDGAR reader and its fair-access hold. The Listen cassette uses this browser's built-in speech to read available headlines and publishers; it does not claim full-article audio or a licensed audio feed. The channel does not place trades or infer that a headline predicts price direction.

Historical pattern matching, publisher subscriptions, and full video or article playback require separately verified sources and their own owner contracts.

## Reference features to evaluate

- thinkorswim's [Live News](https://toslc.thinkorswim.com/center/howToTos/thinkManual/Left-Sidebar/Live-News) filters by symbol, watchlist or keyword and links the selected symbol to other tools. Market Compass currently shares one selected symbol across its channel and analysis; watchlist-wide feeds and keyword filters remain future work.
- thinkorswim's [Analyze](https://toslc.thinkorswim.com/center/howToTos/thinkManual/Analyze) includes simulated trades, risk profiles, probability analysis and historical option review. Market Compass's theoretical options lab covers a narrower research-only subset.
- Webull's [desktop options practice](https://www.webull.com/learn/courseware/gOYdrM/Practice-Your-Options-Trading-on-Webull-Desktop?courseId=DbpPvC&sourcePage=InvestEducationLessonList) and [strategy list](https://www.webull.com/help/faq/10980-Available-Options-Strategies) provide references for broader virtual strategies. Their paper fills do not prove live trade realism in Market Compass.

Upcoming earnings require a company-announced date or a clearly labeled estimate. Congressional transaction disclosures require source matching to a specific issuer and the report date; a report can be delayed and does not establish a current position. The official [House disclosure search](https://disclosures-clerk.house.gov/FinancialDisclosure/ViewSearch) and [SEC API documentation](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-application-programming-interfaces) are starting points, not connected data feeds in this release.
