## Why Guide / question
Does Invo introduce useful research methods that Market Compass should consider, without erasing Market Compass's evidence-first strengths or accidentally building trading execution?

## Invo publisher-described functions
Social trader discovery and following; trader performance/risk scoring; public histories and open positions; copy or 'Mimic' trades; cryptocurrency perpetual/futures trading and leverage, with venue integration. Specific fee/referral/market counts and availability depend on current official product terms; verify before adopting. Profitability claims or historic winning percentages do not prove future returns.

## Extractable innovation
Build an **optional read-only Trader Intelligence lens** only if source rights, legal/privacy review, data acquisition, consistency metrics, and reproducible history can be independently verified. Candidate measures: sample size, realized vs unrealized returns, max drawdown, leverage, concentration, funding/fees, survivorship bias, trade timestamps, regime shifts, copying slippage and trader selection effects. Separate evidence about a person from evidence about an asset. Never infer real-world identity from a public pseudonym. Any trader score needs a documented formula and warning that past performance isn't a future guarantee.

## Existing roadmap fit
Improve data provenance and asset-quality research before introducing social profiles. Evaluate whether features belong as Market Compass lenses or another owner-controlled read-only imported dataset. No duplicate social network or copied-trading venue.

## Decision
**RESEARCH CANDIDATE: social trader-history analysis and transparent risk consistency methodology.** No external account connection, copy trading, order execution or score influence. Exact Invo API/data license, independent figures and live installed status not verified.
