# Evidence and Signals

## Research decision gates — PARTIAL

Go means the admitted evidence meets the research gates. Watch means relevant evidence is developing or incomplete. Do Not Go means a hard stop or failed gate prevents readiness. Invalidated means the observed setup no longer satisfies its invalidation condition. Notable events are observations that can coexist with any readiness state.

Read Player reality, source freshness, confidence, directional edge, higher-timeframe agreement, route, reward/risk, missing inputs, and counter-evidence. A hard stop takes precedence over a favorable bull/bear split. In the [real Door AAPL readiness example](http://market-compass.localhost/api/decision-readiness?symbol=AAPL), inspect the evidence split beside the hard stops and gate results. On November 14, 2026, AAPL showed 80 bullish and 20 bearish evidence, with confidence 0.598 against a 0.65 minimum. Missing market capitalization also triggered the evidence-gap hard stop. The resulting state was Do Not Go. Current output changes with market observations.

Keep the input snapshot, formula or rule, timeframe, timestamps, source/provenance, confidence, limiting evidence, and export reference together. Universal receipt coverage remains a commissioning debt. See the [decision-gates design](../../../docs/DECISION-GATES-DESIGN.md).
