# Use Market Compass

## Analysis and navigation — PARTIAL

Search by symbol or company, select a result, and run the analysis. Open the route to inspect the last and next Bus Stops, invalidation, and downside structure. Select an available timeframe in the current view and verify the observation window in the resulting evidence. Use Appearance for System, Light, or Dark. Use the right rail and Menu to switch rooms and recover the layout. Open an Actor from the current report and export its receipt from the comp card.

## Research rooms — PARTIAL

Open [intelTHAT](http://market-compass.localhost/intelthat/) to run the Market Pack, Macro Conditions, News Intelligence, or SEC Monitor. Inspect source, freshness, and formula, then export the receipt. Run History is browser-local. In Market Exchange, inspect catalog contracts and API/auth states. Enable, save, install, SSO, and API mutations stay with the recorded owners. In Measurement Studio, choose a method and an authorized example, review held or calculated output, and export its inputs and receipt. Source Refinery creates local provenance receipts from a repository, URL, pasted text, or file. The file-picker witness is still open. Check the [operator status guide](../../../docs/STATUS.md) for the current limits.
