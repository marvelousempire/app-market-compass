# Research v1 Delivery

## Research v1 delivery program — APPROVED TARGET

Keep one outcome ledger keyed by phase, exact revision, evidence, remaining work, owner, and real target state. The following checks are acceptance criteria.

| Phase | Required acceptance evidence |
| --- | --- |
| 1. Close 20/20 commissioning | Exercise isolated Stop, restart and Door recovery, real file-picker intake, the producer receipt census, and cross-owner Studio truth. |
| 2. Data-only Alpaca gateway | Prove read-only market data and negative order, account, and funding probes. Measure request budget and fail closed on entitlement loss. |
| 3. Delayed whole-market shadow scan | Validate supported universe, SIP provenance, at least 15-minute age, seven intervals, and failover, backfill and replay. |
| 4. Live IEX focus-30 shadow lane | Prove four pinned choices, governed rotation of up to 26 slots, cap under reconnect, and the IEX-only final Go prohibition. |
| 5. Persistent workspaces and signals | Prove restart restoration, immutable run manifests, receipt export, idempotent replay, and receipt coverage. |
| 6. Event Watch | Replay an early report through primary confirmation, correction, retraction, and resolution in one incident. Verify provenance and direct/thematic labels. |
| 7. Controlled iMessage delivery | Prove opt-in, revocation, tier selection, any-session delivery, replay deduplication, and Relay Texts receipts. |
| 8. Market Compass Studio correction | Read back version, commissioned state, health route, reachability, and the disposition of CCK lanes in the real Studio target. |
| 9. Exact-head release and replacement closeout | Pass owner tests, required checks, independent review, protected merge, mirror equality, exact install and named Door readback. Publish the replacement closeout with dated evidence. |

Measure provider publication to first observation, first observation to classification and inbox, and eligible transition to Relay receipt. The objectives are prompt first detection after source availability, immediate processing of eligible state changes within the measured service budget, and transparent degradation when feeds are stale or backlogged. Set numeric budgets from shadow-lane evidence before promotion and record percentiles, gaps, and receipt times. SIP retains the mandatory minimum delay.

## Outside the Research v1 program — PLANNED LATER

Advanced model comparison and calibrated outcome models require separate evidence and admission.

## Broker and financial effects — PERMANENTLY PROHIBITED

Broker credentials, orders, execution, positions, and money movement remain excluded from every phase.
