# Actors and Instruments

## Report-bound Actor spaces — PARTIAL

Open Omo, Prince, Wuda, or Brown’s Barometers from a report. Each Actor’s comp card identifies the role, its own psychology, cited inputs, and an exportable receipt bound to the current report. Persistent editing of Actor profiles remains a target.

- **Omo** covers catalysts, news, event risk, and counter-evidence.
- **Prince** covers Bus Stops, routes, reward/risk, and invalidation.
- **Wuda** covers trend, momentum, freshness, and directional RSI crossings.
- **Brown’s Barometers** owns measurement and formulas, including RSI, MACD, ATR, Bollinger Bands, moving averages, and other technical instruments.

Wuda records upward and downward RSI crossings at 35, 50, and 65. Compare the previous and current RSI to determine direction and retain the observation timeframe, freshness, source, and values. Brown’s Barometers owns measurement and technical instruments, including Measurement Studio. Marvin’s Metrics was removed and its useful measurements consolidated under Brown.
