# Start

## Research workbench — PARTIAL

Open the [Market Compass Door](http://market-compass.localhost/). Start on BB or choose AAPL, QQQ, or SPCX. An edited watchlist retains its own choices. Type a company name such as Apple, choose AAPL from the suggestions, and run a report. Check the provider and timestamp before interpreting the result.

The bullish and bearish shares describe the relative weight of admitted evidence and add to 100. They do not estimate the chance of profit or the chance that price reaches the next stop. Confidence is a separate assessment.

## Financial authority — PERMANENTLY PROHIBITED

Market Compass cannot accept broker credentials, manage positions, record trades, create or execute orders, or move money. Read the [status contract](../../../docs/STATUS.md), [commissioning receipt](../../../docs/receipts/CLOSEOUT-REVIEW-2026-11-12.json), and [live operator status](http://market-compass.localhost/api/operator/status) together.
