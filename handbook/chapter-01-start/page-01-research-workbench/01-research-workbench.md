# Start

## Research workbench — LIVE

Open the [Market Compass Door](http://market-compass.localhost/). The left rail chooses a room; the right rail shows that room's sections. The center shows the selected work. Longer evidence scrolls within that work area. On a narrow screen, use Menu to reach rooms and their sections.

Start with BB or choose AAPL, QQQ, or SPCX. Each ticker is a Player in the Exchange. Choose a Player card to open its individual Record, then open its Market Studio for the dated price tape and research rooms. An edited watchlist retains its own choices. Type a company name such as Apple, choose AAPL from the suggestions, then press **Analyze**. While the report runs, the status says *Analyzing*. When it finishes, Compass Desk opens the result. Check the provider and timestamp before interpreting it. If analysis fails, read the visible error and try a supported symbol; a prior report is not proof of a new result.

The bullish and bearish shares describe the relative weight of admitted evidence and add to 100. They do not estimate the chance of profit or the chance that price reaches the next stop. Confidence is a separate assessment.

## Financial authority — PERMANENTLY PROHIBITED

Market Compass cannot accept brokerage credentials, manage a real account position, submit or execute a real order, or move money. Options Lab can record **virtual** orders, positions, and a journal in this browser. Those records are simulations, never broker activity. Read the [status contract](../../../docs/STATUS.md), [historical commissioning receipt](../../../docs/receipts/CLOSEOUT-REVIEW-2026-11-12.json), and [live operator status](http://market-compass.localhost/api/operator/status) together.
